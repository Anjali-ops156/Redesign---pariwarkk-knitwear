About Panwar Knitwear

A knitwear house rooted in Ludhiana’s textile tradition.

We are the factory. Fabric, print and stitching happen under our own roof in Ludhiana, so specifications and pricing come straight from the source.

Who We Are

Panwar Knitwear is a leading manufacturer based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India, specializing in high-quality knitwear. We offer a diverse range of products including T-shirts, track pants, lowers, shorts, sweatshirts, and hoodies under our brands ZONIXA and MSP Sports.

Our Craftsmanship

Our garments are made from premium fabrics such as Spun Fleece, Dry Fit, Honeycomb Lycra, 100% Cotton, Cotton Lycra, NS Bonded, Russian Fleece, and Sherpa. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with the latest technology to ensure high-quality, comfortable, and sustainable products.

Our Leadership

Founded by Mohar Singh Panwar, with Prabhu Panwar and Bhala Ram Panwar as co-founders and CEOs, and online presence managed by Rohitash Panwar, Panwar Knitwear is committed to excellence and maintaining our reputation as a top manufacturer in India.

Manufacturing Process

From fabric roll to finished carton.

  1. 01

    Fabric Selection

    Every order starts with the right knit. We work with Spun Fleece, Dry Fit, Honeycomb Lycra, 100% Cotton, Cotton Lycra, NS Bonded, Russian Fleece and Sherpa — matched to the garment and the season.

  2. 02

    Knitting & Fabric Preparation

    Fabric is prepared to the required weight — up to the 320 GSM winter-weight fleece used in our heavy hoodies — then checked for hand-feel, shade and consistency before cutting.

  3. 03

    Cutting

    Panels are cut to the article specification across the full size range so every piece in a bulk run matches the approved sample.

  4. 04

    Printing & Branding

    Chest prints, sleeve prints, digital prints and applied logos — all produced in-house, as seen throughout the ZONIXA range.

  5. 05

    Stitching & Assembly

    Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with the latest technology to produce high-quality garments efficiently and sustainably.

  6. 06

    Rigorous Quality Checks

    Our products undergo rigorous quality checks to ensure they meet the highest standards — stitching, print placement, measurement and finish.

  7. 07

    Packing & Dispatch

    Finished goods are packed by size ratio and dispatched for bulk orders across India.
